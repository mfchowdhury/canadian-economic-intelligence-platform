THE ECONOMIC PROBLEM

Canadian economic data is valuable, but fragmented.

Canadian economic indicators are published across multiple official sources, frequencies, formats, and release schedules. This makes it difficult to compare economic conditions quickly across growth, inflation, labour markets, housing, fiscal policy, and other areas.

The Canadian Economic Intelligence Platform brings these datasets into a consistent analytical environment so they can be validated, integrated, monitored, forecast, and communicated more efficiently.

KEY CAPABILITIES

Built across the full data and analytics lifecycle.

The platform combines data engineering, analytical modeling, forecasting, reporting, and interactive delivery in one solution.

01

Automated Data Ingestion

Parameterized Azure Data Factory pipelines ingest official economic data from multiple Canadian public sources.

02

Lakehouse & Data Quality

Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers provide raw history, validated datasets, quarantine handling, and analytical outputs.

03

Economic Data Integration

Economic indicators with different frequencies and structures are aligned into consistent analytical datasets.

04

Analytics & Forecasting

Gold analytical models support economic comparisons, derived indicators, forecasting features, and model evaluation.

05

Power BI Reporting

Azure SQL serves curated reporting tables to a multi-page Power BI dashboard for economic analysis and forecasting.

06

Interactive Demo & AI-Ready Layer

Streamlit and structured analytical outputs provide a foundation for interactive exploration and future AI-assisted analysis.
