LAKEHOUSE DESIGN

Bronze, Silver, and Gold separate raw history from validated and analytical data.

The medallion architecture provides clear data ownership, supports reproducibility, and prevents analytical logic from being mixed with raw ingestion.

01

Bronze

Immutable raw data is preserved in its original source-aligned structure for traceability and reprocessing.

  • Raw source history
  • Minimal transformation
  • Original source structure
  • Replay and audit support
→
02

Silver

Source datasets are validated, standardized, cleaned, and prepared for consistent downstream integration.

  • Schema validation
  • Canonical transformations
  • Business-rule checks
  • Quarantine handling
→
03

Gold

Validated indicators are aligned by analytical purpose and transformed into reusable economic intelligence datasets.

  • Economic overview
  • Regional analysis
  • Industry analysis
  • Fiscal and affordability analysis
  • Forecasting features

ANALYTICAL LAYER

Economic indicators are aligned by frequency, geography, and analytical purpose.

Economic data arrives at different frequencies and levels of detail. The Gold layer standardizes these differences so the datasets can support integrated analysis without changing the original source data.

Monthly Indicators CPI, labour, retail sales, GDP

Preserved at monthly analytical grain where available.

Daily Indicators USD/CAD exchange rate

Aggregated to monthly measures for integrated economic analysis.

Policy Indicators Bank of Canada policy rate

Aligned using period-end values where appropriate.

Lower-Frequency Data Population and fiscal indicators

Integrated according to their published reporting frequency and analytical use.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Architecture decisions prioritize maintainability, validation, and analytical clarity.

01

Preserve Raw History

Bronze datasets remain source-aligned so upstream changes can be investigated and transformations can be reproduced.

02

Validate Before Integration

Silver processing applies validation and quality rules before data enters analytical models.

03

Separate Processing from Serving

Databricks performs analytical transformation while Azure SQL provides a stable interface for reporting.

04

Build Reusable Analytical Models

Gold datasets are organized by economic question rather than by source system alone.
