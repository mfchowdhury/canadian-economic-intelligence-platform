REPORTING PRINCIPLES

The dashboard emphasizes clarity, traceability, and analytical consistency.

01

Curated Data First

Power BI consumes reporting-ready SQL tables rather than performing raw data engineering inside the report.

02

Consistent Analytical Grain

Visuals are built on datasets whose frequency and business keys are aligned upstream.

03

Evidence Alongside Forecasts

Forecast outputs are presented with model comparison and holdout evaluation rather than as isolated values.

04

Decision-Focused Layout

Report pages are organized around economic questions rather than the structure of the source systems.
