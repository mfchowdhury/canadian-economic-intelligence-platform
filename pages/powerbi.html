THE ECONOMIC PROBLEM

Official economic data is powerful, but fragmented.

Canadian economic indicators are published across different official sources, frequencies, structures, and release schedules. Comparing economic conditions across growth, inflation, labour markets, housing, fiscal policy, and other areas therefore requires substantial preparation.

The platform brings these sources into a consistent analytical environment where data can be validated, standardized, integrated, monitored, analyzed, forecast, and communicated more efficiently.

CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineering, analytics, and delivery in one platform.

The project demonstrates the complete path from official source data to validated analytical products and interactive decision tools.

01

Automated Data Engineering

Azure Data Factory orchestrates source ingestion and Databricks processing through a repeatable cloud workflow.

02

Lakehouse & Data Quality

Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers preserve raw history, validate source data, quarantine invalid records, and produce reusable analytical outputs.

03

Economic Analytics & Forecasting

Integrated economic indicators support national, regional, industry, fiscal, affordability, and time-series forecasting analysis.

04

Reporting & Interactive Intelligence

Azure SQL serves curated outputs to Power BI reporting and a Streamlit application for live automation and data-grounded economic analysis.
