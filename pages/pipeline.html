DEPENDENCY & FAILURE CONTROL

Downstream processing depends on successful upstream execution.

The master workflow uses dependency-based execution so required ingestion and Silver activities complete before Gold processing begins. Explicit failure paths surface unsuccessful activities and prevent dependent success-path activities from continuing.

Upstream Ingestion completes first

Required source data must be available before its Silver transformation starts.

Validation Silver precedes Gold

Gold activities depend on validated upstream processing rather than raw ingestion alone.

Failure Explicit failure paths

Failed activities are surfaced and prevent the dependent success path from continuing.
