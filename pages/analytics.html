RETAIL SALES FORECASTING

Multiple forecasting approaches were evaluated against a common holdout period.

Five models were compared using out-of-sample error metrics. Seasonal Naive produced the lowest validation RMSE and was selected for the final one-month forecast.

MODEL SELECTION

Validation performance determines the final model.

Candidate models were evaluated against a holdout period using MAE and RMSE. The final model was selected using the lowest validation RMSE rather than choosing a model based on complexity.

ANALYTICAL PRINCIPLES

Economic interpretation begins with consistent data design.

01

Align Frequencies

Indicators with different publication frequencies are aligned according to analytical purpose.

02

Preserve Economic Meaning

Transformations retain the meaning and grain of official source indicators.

03

Evaluate Out of Sample

Forecast models are compared using holdout performance rather than training fit alone.

04

Separate Analysis from Presentation

Analytical logic is created upstream and served to reporting applications through curated datasets.
