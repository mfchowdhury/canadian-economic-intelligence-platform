RETAIL SALES FORECASTING

Forecasting provides a complete model-selection and evaluation case study.

Four candidate models were compared using expanding-window one-step-ahead validation. SARIMA achieved the lowest validation RMSE, was then evaluated on a separate 12-month holdout period, and was used for the final one-month forecast.

MODEL SELECTION

Validation performance determines the final model.

Candidate models were compared using expanding-window validation with MAE and RMSE. The model with the lowest validation RMSE was selected and then evaluated on a separate 12-month holdout period.

ANALYTICAL PRINCIPLES

Economic interpretation begins with consistent data design.

01

Align Frequencies

Indicators with different publication frequencies are aligned according to analytical purpose.

02

Preserve Economic Meaning

Transformations retain the meaning, units, and analytical grain of official source indicators.

03

Evaluate Out of Sample

Forecast models are compared using out-of-sample validation, with the selected model evaluated on a separate holdout period.

04

Separate Analysis from Presentation

Analytical logic is created upstream and served to reporting applications through curated datasets.
